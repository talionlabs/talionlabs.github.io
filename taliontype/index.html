What is WPM?

Words Per Minute measures how fast you type. One "word" equals five characters including spaces. Your WPM score reflects correct five-character groups typed per minute.

What is a good typing speed?

The average adult types around 40 WPM. 65–80 WPM is proficient for office work. Programmers typically aim for 80–100 WPM, and professional typists often exceed 120 WPM.

How to improve typing speed?

Use daily challenges, focus on accuracy before speed, and review the error breakdown to target weak keys. Consistent 10–15 minute sessions will show improvement within weeks.
